# chainlink: Chain-Linked Price and Volume Measures

Tools to derive price and volume measures of national accounts following
chapter 8 of the International Monetary Fund's Quarterly National
Accounts Manual: Laspeyres-type, Paasche-type and Fisher-type volume
indices, chain-linking of quarterly or monthly volume indices with the
annual overlap and the one-quarter overlap techniques, exact
contributions to percent change of the chain-linked volume measures, and
non-additivity of the chain-linked series. The benchmarking of the
quarterly series on annual data can be done with the 'tempdisagg'
package.
